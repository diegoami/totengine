Wire the stored tile/improvement, unit visibility and city knowledge/size
projection into a versioned session recipe. Verify initial map/view/choices,
zero accepted-order snapshot state, repeated views and checkpoint restore
without hidden-state disclosure. Declare and test the selected engine policy
and subsequent knowledge/replay normalization; do not silently reuse a changed
recipe for old checkpoints. Then complete the stock resource/record/phase
admission checks and the #198 client route against that recipe.

The contract explicitly selects an **engine** opponent/configuration/seed
policy and does not claim original AI mission/cache/shared-RNG continuation.
Full native AI remains the separate #117 program with its existing client
gate. Broader movement, cargo, conversation, direct-reveal and city/AI-cache
lifecycle work must be assessed against the admitted profile and declared
normalization, rather than automatically made universal import prerequisites.
This does not waive any frozen predicate: unsupported required input behavior,
unknown fog/pending state or incomplete dependencies still reject, and no
privileged human driver or hidden-state leak is approved.

This is implementation progress, not milestone/release acceptance. The owner
launches milestone reviews and accepts the new route on both clients.
