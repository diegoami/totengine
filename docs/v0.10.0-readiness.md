## Remaining material differences

The full native AI has not been ported. Production agreement percentages compare raw intentions before the existing
obsolete-build upgrade, so a unit-ID mismatch need not mean a different unit
is completed. The percentages are descriptive; no statistical acceptance budget was frozen. The archived
standard inputs are holdout positions for this calibration, not controlled
all-AI original campaigns. Native research/production checks prepare saved
inputs and do not prove original full-turn RNG/statistics equivalence.

- #112: advanced settlers, terrain-development orders and scenario production.
  FallRome's 5.6% production agreement remains a substantial gap.
- #113: capital relocation/civil war, technology plunder, full capture
  diplomacy/civilization consequences, callback/cache/cargo edge cases and
  native immediate production. Capture currently requires the explicit
  `occupy` order; legacy `move` logs retain their earlier meaning.
- #114: naval/air/transport missions, seams and large-scenario scheduling/cost.
  The inherited 200-order per-civ cap can leave large armies unprocessed.
  FallRome is much slower than the standard position under both policies.
- #115/#96: government/rate choices, treaties/declarations of war and
  diplomacy-aware targeting. Current routing treats foreign visible presence
  as possible targets; research intentionally lacks private foreign caches.
- #95/#97: complete native turn parity and victory/scoring remain separate.
  Engine visibility, campaign initialization and outcome conditions retain
  their previously documented limits.

These follow-ups have no assigned release. They do not waive #106; the
independent reviewer must assess whether the supported opponent meets its
acceptance despite the stated limits. v0.11 starts original UI
workflow discovery, owner-reviewed design and client milestone planning
(#107, decision 0005). Playable clients span later milestones; pluggable AIs
come afterwards. This candidate implements no playable-client UI.

`docs/evidence/ai-research-*.json`, `ai-production-comparison.json`,
`ai-production-choices.txt`, `capture-kernels.json`,
`capture-kernel-vectors.txt` and `builtin-observer.json`. The observer screenshot

cargo test -p engine --test generated_campaign --release -- --include-ignored
cargo build -p engine --example ai_policy_audit --release
CIVTOT_HOME=/path/to/CIVTOT python3 tools/ai_campaign_audit.py \
  --engine-example target/release/examples/ai_policy_audit \

nightly test intentionally ignored, five Python report tests, the complete
